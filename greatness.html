The Rolls-Royce Story

Inspiring Greatness is not merely a tagline — it is the philosophy that has guided Rolls-Royce since its founding. A confluence of exceptional and extraordinary narratives, this is the story of individuals who dared to dream beyond the ordinary and create something truly timeless.

From the Spirit of Ecstasy that graces every bonnet to the whisper-quiet cabins that have become the benchmark of automotive luxury, every Rolls-Royce carries within it the spirit of innovation, artistry, and an unwavering commitment to being the very best in the world.

Heritage Timeline

1904
The Meeting

Charles Rolls and Henry Royce meet at the Midland Hotel, Manchester — the birth of a legend.

1907
Silver Ghost

The 40/50 HP — later named Silver Ghost — is hailed as "the best car in the world."

1911
Spirit of Ecstasy

The iconic Spirit of Ecstasy mascot is designed by sculptor Charles Sykes.

2003
Home of Rolls-Royce

The new Rolls-Royce headquarters opens at Goodwood, England — a purpose-built temple of luxury.

2023
Spectre — Electric Future

Rolls-Royce Spectre becomes the brand's first fully electric motor car, beginning a new era.
