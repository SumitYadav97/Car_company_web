About The Ghost

The Rolls-Royce Ghost is the purest expression of Rolls-Royce yet. Born from a desire to create a slightly smaller, less ostentatious means to own a Rolls-Royce, Ghost has gone on to become the most successful model in the marque's history.

With its flawless performance and superior craftsmanship, this distinguished masterpiece was designed to exhilarate. The Ghost combines minimalist design philosophy with extraordinary engineering to deliver an experience of effortless luxury that is unmatched in the automotive world.
